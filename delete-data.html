Delete Your Data — PickleScore

PickleScore stores your data only on your device. We do not operate a server, so there is no copy of your match, player, or statistics data held by us to delete.

How to delete your data

  1. Open PickleScore on your device.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Clear All Data and confirm. This permanently deletes all matches, players, game records, point logs, and settings from the App's local database.

Alternatively, uninstalling PickleScore deletes the App's local database entirely.

Exported files

If you exported a CSV file or a backup file, those files live wherever you saved or shared them (for example, your Downloads folder or your email). The App cannot delete files outside its own storage — please delete those files yourself.

Data held by Google

Advertising data (Google AdMob) and purchase records (Google Play Billing) are held by Google, not by us. To manage or delete that data, use your Google account settings:

  • Ad personalization: your device Settings → Google → Ads, or adssettings.google.com.
  • Purchase history: Google Play → your profile → Payments & subscriptions.
