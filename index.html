Privacy Policy — PickleScore

Last updated: November 19, 2026

PickleScore ("the App") is developed by ASEMedia Tech ("we", "us"). This Privacy Policy explains what data the App collects, how it is used, and your choices. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

1. Data stored on your device

The App is offline-first. The match data you create — match scores, player names, game records, point logs, statistics, and your settings — is stored only on your device in a local database. We do not operate a server that receives this data, and we cannot see it.

If you use the CSV export or backup feature, the file is created on your device for you to share or keep. We do not receive a copy.

2. Location data

The App does not collect, use, or store your location. It requests no location permission.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the App displays ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use the following data to serve and measure ads:

  • Advertising ID (Android Advertising ID)
  • Device information (device model, operating system version)
  • Approximate location (derived from IP address) for ad targeting
  • App interaction data (ad impressions and clicks)

This data is collected by Google, not by us, and is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. You can opt out of personalized advertising in your device Settings → Google → Ads ("Opt out of Ads Personalization") or by resetting your Advertising ID. The App does not share your match, player, or statistics data with AdMob or any advertiser. PickleScore Pro subscribers see no ads.

4. In-app purchases (PickleScore Pro)

Optional PickleScore Pro subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. We do not collect, see, or store your payment information — payments are handled entirely by Google under Google's Privacy Policy. The App stores only whether your device currently holds an active Pro entitlement, so it can unlock Pro features; this status is verified with Google Play.

5. Data we do not collect

We do not collect, store, or share your name, email address, phone number, financial account details, or government identifiers. The App requires no account and no sign-in. Player names you enter are stored only on your device.

6. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data. The only third parties that receive data from the App are Google AdMob (Section 3) and Google Play Billing (Section 4), as described above. Your match, player, and statistics data is never shared with any third party by the App.

7. Data retention and deletion

Your data lives on your device for as long as you keep the App installed. You can delete all of your data at any time from Settings → Clear All Data. Uninstalling the App deletes the local database (note: exported CSV or backup files you saved elsewhere remain until you delete them). See our data deletion page for details.

8. Children's privacy

The App is intended for adults (18+). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us and we will help remove it.

9. Security

Because your match and statistics data never leaves your device, there is no server-side breach risk for that data. AdMob and Google Play Billing data handling is governed by Google's security practices.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the App after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at asemediatech@gmail.com.

PickleScore is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any pickleball organization or rating system. The Skill Rating shown in the App is an informal estimate calculated on your device; it is not an official rating.
